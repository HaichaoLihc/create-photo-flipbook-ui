# Image generation for spreads

Image generation is available when the chosen style calls for transformed artwork. The intended result is a coherent book that expresses the requested style or reference, with purposeful variation across spreads.

- A complete double-page artwork supports composition across the gutter; keep important content clear of the fold.
- In an outside-cover artwork, the back cover is on the left and the front cover on the right.
- Consistent page proportions and intact source/reference associations support assembly and later revisions.

Choose the generation and revision process to suit the design. [Contact sheets](../scripts/make_contact_sheet.py) are available for comparing artwork; [runtime notes](book-editing.md) cover display in the book.
